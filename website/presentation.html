Clinical Background

Why ADHD Diagnosis Needs Machine Learning

01 · High Global Burden

ADHD impacts 5% to 7% of children worldwide, impairing executive function, academic attainment, and emotional regulation.

02 · Subjective & Delayed

Standard assessments rely on parent/teacher interviews and clinician variance. Waitlists exceed 12–18 months in community healthcare.

03 · Masking & Gender Gap

High-IQ children and females frequently mask overt hyperactivity, exhibiting subtle inattention and suffering from delayed diagnosis.

04 · Consortium Site Bias

Multicenter cohorts exhibit massive clinic prevalence shifts (0% to 58%), causing naive AI models to memorize hospital names instead of pathology.

Project Scope

What Exactly Was Built & Delivered

Data Ingestion

Harmonized 10 clinical centers, pruned 101 duplicate records, normalized Conners & ADHD-RS to [0,1], and fixed sentinel NaNs.

Statistical Rigor

Evaluated Cohen's d effect sizes, Welch's t-tests, Mann-Whitney U, and VIFs across demographic and cognitive markers.

13-Model Zoo

Trained and tuned 13 ML models via 5-fold CV, randomized search, voting/stacking ensembles, and Youden thresholding.

Fairness & Bias

Conducted Leave-One-Site-Out (LOSO) checks, audited 0.70 site confounder, and uncovered ethical gender sensitivity disparity.

SHAP Attribution

Decomposed black-box decisions with SHAP beeswarm/force plots, uncovering and fixing the NYU handedness artifact.

Connectomics & App

Extracted fMRI connectomes with Nilearn (MSDL & BASC) and built an interactive 3D WebGL web app with live in-browser inference.

Phase 1 · ETL Pipeline

Multi-Center Clinical Data Harmonization

  • Ingested 10 Clinics: Downloaded raw TSVs directly from the public ADHD-200 S3 repository.
  • Excised 101 Duplicates: Filtered repeated rows to ensure zero data leakage across splits.
  • Normalized Questionnaires: Unified Conners' T-scores (40–100) and ADHD-RS IV (18–72) onto a continuous [0, 1] severity scale.
  • Harmonized Handedness: Converted disparate categorical & continuous scales into a unified dominant hand indicator.
  • Quarantined Brown Site: Isolated unlabelled cohort (n=26) for zero-shot testing.
Phase 2 · Exploratory Analysis

Statistical Findings & Effect Sizes (Cohen's d)

  • Sex Disparity: Boys diagnosed at more than double the rate of girls (49% vs 22%).
  • Cognitive Shifts: Verbal IQ (d = -0.47), Performance IQ (d = -0.55), and Full IQ (d = -0.56) show modest drops but heavy overlap.
  • Symptom Dominance: Parent rating questionnaires exhibit massive clinical separation (Cohen's d = 2.87, p < 1e-100).
  • Site Asymmetry: ADHD rates vary between 0% (WashU) and 58% (NYU), acting as a primary statistical confounder.
  • PCA & t-SNE: Proved no distinct biological clusters exist without symptom ratings.
Phase 3 · Feature Engineering

Evidence-Based Preprocessing & Class Balancing

  • Stratified 80/20 Split: Locked 20% holdout test set (n=187 for Set A; n=98 for Set B) untouched until final evaluation.
  • Imputation Benchmark: Mean, Median, KNN, and Iterative imputation gave identical AUCs; median was selected for operational simplicity.
  • Class Imbalance Mitigation: Class-weighted loss raised minority catch rate (sensitivity) from 49% to 67%.
  • Non-Linear Features: Engineered Verbal-Performance IQ Discrepancy and quadratic Age curve (Age - 12)^2.
  • Consensus Feature Selection: RFECV and L1 Lasso confirmed parent symptom ratings outrank all other features.
Phase 4 · Model Zoo

Benchmarking 13 Machine Learning Architectures

  • 13 Architectures Evaluated: Logistic Regression, LDA, Gaussian NB, KNN, Random Forest, Extra Trees, GBDT, XGBoost, LightGBM, AdaBoost, RBF SVM, Voting, and Stacking.
  • Rigorous 5-Fold Cross Validation: Evaluated all models under identical folds with full pipeline containment.
  • Hyperparameter Tuning: Conducted RandomizedSearchCV across model parameter distributions.
  • Youden Thresholding: Calibrated decision cutoffs to balance sensitivity and specificity under class imbalance.

Phase 6 · Algorithmic Fairness

Uncovering Severe Gender Disparity in Set A

  • Severe Catch-Rate Gap: In Set A (demographics only), sensitivity for boys was 75.0%, but collapsed to 25.0% for girls!
  • Why? The model learned that "male" is a major clue, codifying historical referral bias.
  • Clinical Danger: Girls with high IQ or inattentive profiles were systematically misclassified as neurotypical.
  • Resolution in Set B: Incorporating parent questionnaires restored female sensitivity to 100.0%!
Phase 7 · Confounder Auditing

Clinic Confounder Discovery & LOSO Validation

  • The Clinic Shortcut: A model provided ONLY the clinic ID reaches 0.7025 AUC without looking at the patient!
  • Strict Exclusion: Clinic ID was excluded from production features to ensure genuine generalization.
  • Leave-One-Site-Out (LOSO): Iteratively trained on N-1 clinics and tested on the remaining clinic.
  • Performance Swings: Scores range from 0.56 AUC (KKI) to 0.86 AUC (Peking), proving multi-site re-calibration is mandatory.
Phase 8 · Explainable AI

SHAP Feature Attribution & The Handedness Glitch

  • The Handedness Mystery: Handedness originally ranked 2nd in importance for Set A. Left-handedness pushed strongly toward ADHD.
  • Investigating Raw Data: NYU (58% ADHD rate) coded handedness as continuous Edinburgh scores, while other clinics coded binary flags!
  • The Fix: Re-harmonized all clinics to true dominant hand -> Handedness attribution collapsed to zero!
  • Takeaway: Proved that explainable AI (SHAP) is essential to detect hidden data collection artifacts.
Phase 9 · Neuroimaging Analysis

Resting-State fMRI Connectomes via Nilearn

  • Connectome Pipeline (n = 40): MSDL (39 regions) and BASC-64 atlases; Correlation, Partial Correlation, and Tangent Space.
  • 42 Configurations Tested: Top model: PCA + RBF SVM on MSDL correlation matrix (Accuracy: 56.2%, ROC-AUC: 0.63).
  • 500-Iteration Permutation Test: Observed score yields p = 0.11 (Null mean = 51.2%).
  • Scientific Verdict: In n=40 samples, rs-fMRI connectomes do NOT significantly outperform random guessing. Larger cohorts (n>2,000) are needed.

Conclusion & Impact

Key Scientific Takeaways & Future Directions

What the Project Proved
  • Demographics + IQ establish an upper bound of ~0.73 AUC.
  • Parent surveys dominate diagnosis (~0.96 AUC, 94% sensitivity).
  • Demographic AI discriminates against females (25% catch rate).
  • SHAP explainability is mandatory to catch multi-site data artifacts.
  • Small-sample fMRI connectomes are limited by sample size.
Future Enhancements
  • Scale neuroimaging to the ABCD Study (n > 11,800).
  • Model teacher-parent rating discordance as an informative feature.
  • Incorporate Polygenic Risk Scores (PRS) for gene-cognition fusion.
  • Package into HL7 FHIR-compliant Electronic Health Record (EHR) clinical decision support modules.
